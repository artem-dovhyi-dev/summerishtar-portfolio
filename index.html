About me

I love good stories, so my work feels crafted, strategic and human, whether it’s sitting on a supermarket shelf or scaling across digital screens.

I started out in 2018 in animation and concept art, first at Pencell Animation Studio and then illustrating public health campaigns for UNICEF Myanmar. From there I moved into fast-paced agency and B2B/B2C brand roles across Southeast Asia. Agency life taught me speed and structure: several client accounts at once, and up to six campaign visuals a day.

Today I’m based in Bangkok. I’ve worked with the PepsiCo creative team on launches for Lay’s and Quaker, and led branding and pitch decks for Siam University’s Global Academy across eight markets, where my template system cut campaign turnaround from seven days to three. Now I run my own practice, Ishtar Design, building brand guidelines, packaging, digital UI and campaigns with Adobe Creative Suite, Figma and AI-assisted workflows. I’m happiest when a project needs all of them.
