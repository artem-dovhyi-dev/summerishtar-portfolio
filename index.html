About me

I love good stories, so my work tends to feel hand-made, emotional and personal, even when it’s sitting on a supermarket shelf.

I started out in 2018 as an intern at Pencell Animation Studio, colouring scenes and designing characters, and grew into a lead concept artist. Agency life at Idea Planet taught me speed: six visuals a day, 22 brands in a month.

Today I’m based in Bangkok, where I’ve worked with the PepsiCo creative team on launches like Lay’s Sour & Spicy Extra Chili. I design packaging, brand systems and illustrations, and I’m happiest when a project lets me do all three.
